Every idea in this book is motivated by a **system** — a real thing you are
trying to control — and it unfolds as a journey of questions, each one nudging
you toward the next idea. *Wouldn't it be cool if it could do that on its own?
Does it still work if I disturb it? What about from a bad start, with no room
for error?*

Pick the system that matches your background. Each one is the same journey told
at a different altitude — same questions, different depth.
